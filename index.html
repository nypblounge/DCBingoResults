Method / Assumptions how the page is built
What comes from the save: team assignment, points, GP, replay claim counts, unique tile counts, claimed tile icons/details, combat level, total level, and Wise Old Man EHP/EHB gains when present.

What comes from the screenshots: draft pick order, pre-bingo OVR, and post-bingo OVR / overall ordering.

Pre rank tie-break: when two players share the same pre-bingo OVR, this page breaks ties alphabetically so rank movement stays deterministic.
